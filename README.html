Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Last updated: 11 October 2026

Current status: The Facebook Page connection and publishing integration are not yet enabled. This policy describes the planned handling of information if you authorize the app to work with a Facebook Page. It must be updated if the actual implementation or data practices differ.

This policy explains how the Gold Market Intelligence service (“the service”) may handle information when an authorized user prepares and publishes gold-market research content to a Facebook Page. The service is operated by the individual listed under Contact.

Information the service may handle

If a Page administrator chooses to connect Facebook, the service may process only information needed for the requested publishing workflow, such as:

  • Basic account and Page details needed to identify the authorized Page and confirm publishing access.
  • Page access credentials supplied through Meta’s authorization flow, used to perform actions the administrator requests.
  • Post text, images, and related publication identifiers or status returned by Meta.

The service’s gold-market reports and graphics may also contain public market-price information obtained from the sources identified in the relevant report. They are not personal information about the Page administrator.

How information is used

Connected Page information and authorization are used to identify the selected Page and, only when requested by an authorized user, prepare or publish that user’s content. The service does not use Page access to read private messages, profile personal information, or publish unrelated content.

Sharing and service providers

The service does not sell personal information. When a user requests Facebook publishing, the relevant post content and image are sent to Meta Platforms, Inc. so Meta can provide the publishing service under its own terms and privacy policy. Hosting or infrastructure providers may process technical information necessary to operate the service. No other sharing is intended unless required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

Storage and retention

As of the effective date, no Facebook integration is connected and the service is not receiving Facebook Page data. Before enabling the integration, the operator will configure where authorization credentials and any publication records are stored, restrict access to them, and set retention periods appropriate to the implementation. The operator will not claim that data is encrypted, retained for a particular period, or deleted automatically unless those controls are actually in place.

Facebook posts published at a user’s request remain on the Page until the Page administrator or Meta removes them. Meta may retain information under Meta’s own policies.

Your choices and deletion requests

A Page administrator can stop future access by removing the app from the Page or revoking its authorization in Facebook settings. To request deletion of information held by the service, email sam2021x@gmail.com from an address you control and identify the Page or information involved. Do not email access tokens or passwords. The operator will respond and delete information the service controls, subject to legal obligations and technical backup or security-log retention.

Security

The operator will use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the deployed service. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other credentials by email or include them in a support request.

Children

The service is intended for adults who manage a Facebook Page. It is not designed for children and does not knowingly seek to collect children’s personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the service’s integration, data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates when the current version was last published. Material changes will be reflected on this page before the changed data practice begins.
